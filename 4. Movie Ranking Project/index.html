The Best Dramas According to Sneha

My Top Dramas of all time

1. The Untamed

The Untamed is a Chinese television series based on the novel Mo Dao Zu Shi by Mo Xiang Tong Xiu. It stars Xiao Zhan and Wang Yibo as the main characters, Wei Wuxian and Lan Wangji, respectively. The series follows the story of Wei Wuxian, a powerful cultivator who becomes a villain after his death and is later resurrected in the body of a young man named Mo Xuanyu. The series explores themes of friendship, loyalty, and the consequences of one's actions. It has gained a large following both in China and internationally for its compelling storyline, strong performances, and beautiful cinematography.

2. When Life Gives You Tangerines

When Life Gives You Tangerines is a Turkish television series that follows the story of a young woman named Elif, who is forced to confront her past and the secrets of her family when she returns to her hometown after many years. The series explores themes of love, betrayal, and the complexities of family relationships. It has been praised for its engaging storyline, strong performances, and emotional depth.

3. The Glory

The Glory is a South Korean television series that tells the story of a woman named Moon Dong-eun, who seeks revenge against her high school bullies after they left her with severe scars. The series delves into themes of bullying, trauma, and the quest for justice. It has been acclaimed for its intense narrative, powerful performances, and its unflinching portrayal of the consequences of bullying.
